From PARC’s public footer and Services page. You do not have to be the competent person for every trade. Same three parts on every job: process, prevention, proof. Method questions go to the Director or the assigned competent person.

How proficient safety needs to be

All PARC services — your depth

I learn PARC’s process and I run it. I support training and prevention in the field, and I keep documentation that proves what we actually did. I am not the asbestos competent person, the lead supervisor, the hydroblast operator, or the infection-control specialist. I walk the job, pause a missing control, and escalate the method.

Walk-level = name the hazard, see if PARC’s control is actually there, pause, document, notify.
Goes up = how to abate, which cartridge, waste code, tank method, biosafety level, UST sampling. Not yours to freelance.

Asbestos abatement Inhalation, dirty clothes/tools, failed containment/decon Walk-level. High attention. Work plan, PPE, respirator status, cards, containment, decon, housekeeping. You do not redesign the method. Covered in hazard #1.
Lead paint abatement Dust, take-home lead, bad containment, kids/occupied buildings Same as asbestos. CDPH method stays with the competent person. You check controls and records.

Surface preparation Hydroblast up to ~40,000 PSI, silica, noise, eye/skin, hose failures Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Exclusion zone, PPE, whip checks, hearing/eye, who is trained on that tool. You do not run the blaster.
Underground storage tanks Vapors, fire/explosion, confined space, contaminated soil, traffic Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Hot work, atmosphere, excavation, PPE. Tank cleaning/entry is confined space until proven otherwise. Waste and sampling methods go up.
Emergency service & response Unknown product, vapor, fire, runoff, wrong PPE, untrained people in the hot zone Walk-level plus your FRO/decon foundation. Size-up, zones, PPE, decon, function matches training. You are not automatically IC or technician. Covered in hazard #3 and the work sample.
Drug lab removal Unknown chemicals, flammables, needles, violence, residual contamination Same as ER unknown. Do not assume identity or PPE. Boundaries, notify, trained people only. Law enforcement/site control is not yours to freelance.
Lab packing Incompatible chemicals, broken containers, wrong class, exposure Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Assigned packers, PPE, segregation. You do not invent DOT classes. Proof is inventory, labels, manifests.
Hazardous waste transportation & disposal Mislabel, incompatible load, spill, traffic, bad manifest Walk-level. High attention on paper. People, packaging, labels, documents, equipment. Waste profile goes up. Covered in hazard #9. PARC is also a listed medical-waste / trauma-scene hauler — same proof rule.
Confined space entry assistance Low O2, toxics, engulfment, failed rescue Walk-level. Hard stop if missing. Permit, testing, ventilation, attendant, comms, rescue, authorized entrants. Covered in hazard #4. You do not interpret the meter unless assigned.
Painting & coatings Solvents, isocyanates, falls, spray overspray, fire Walk-level. Ventilation, respirator status, fall protection, hot work if needed. Coatings chemistry goes up.
Construction shrink wrap Falls, wind, heat, entanglement, fire (shrink tools), access Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Fall protection, weather, fire watch if heat tools, access. Containment still has to be real.
Water restoration Electrical, mold start, slips, contaminated water, confined wet areas Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Power off/lockout as required, PPE, slips, sewage vs clean water is PARC’s call. Escalate category/method.
Dust & infectious disease control Silica/nuisance dust; hospital biosafety, bloodborne, isolation Walk-level. We had not drilled this. Dust: water, barriers, PPE, hearing. Infectious: PARC’s infection-control plan, PPE, waste. You do not set biosafety level. Escalate.
Wildfire disaster remediation (on Services page, not the footer 15) Ash, leftover asbestos/lead, heat, fatigue, debris, traffic, unknown household hazmat Walk-level. Same as debris + hazmat. Heat, PPE, work plan, hidden ACM/lead. You do not invent ash-sampling methods.
