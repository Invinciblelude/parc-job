The job in one paragraph

From the posting

The EHS Specialist supports PARC facilities and operations, implements EHS programs, and is the liaison between employees and internal/external customers. Assists the Director of EHS on audits, incident response, corrective action, injured-employee support, sampling, fit testing, records, workers’ compensation, and ISN/Avetta. Field work: stand, climb, PPE for long periods, lift 50 lb, heat/cold, mechanical equipment, moderate-to-loud noise.
